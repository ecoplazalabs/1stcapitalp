## What happened

India's position in the 2026 World Press Freedom Index has drawn international scrutiny. The country is currently ranked 157th out of 180 countries, reflecting ongoing concerns about media independence and press freedoms.

## Key figures

* **Global ranking:** 157th out of 180 countries in the 2026 World Press Freedom Index

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market operators and PE sponsors evaluating Indian assets or cross-border M&A into India, press freedom metrics signal broader governance and transparency challenges. A lower press freedom ranking typically correlates with reduced information asymmetry disclosure, regulatory opacity, and potential reputational risks for international investors. Institutional investors and acquirers conducting due diligence on Indian targets should factor media independence into their assessment of operational and regulatory risk, particularly where public disclosure and stakeholder accountability are material to deal structure and valuation.
