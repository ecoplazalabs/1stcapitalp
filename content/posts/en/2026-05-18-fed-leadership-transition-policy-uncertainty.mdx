## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Fed policy transitions directly impact financing costs, credit availability and refinancing windows for lower middle-market operators. Cross-border sponsors and acquirers face heightened uncertainty in budgeting for debt service and working capital, particularly in leveraged transactions. Clarity on monetary direction typically emerges gradually during leadership transitions, which may affect M&A timing and deal structuring decisions over the coming quarters. LMM CFOs should monitor Fed communications closely to inform capital allocation and hedging strategies.
