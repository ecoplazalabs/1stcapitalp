## What happened

A Senate ruling has blocked federal funding intended for Secret Service security upgrades associated with a Trump ballroom project. Republicans had sought to allocate $1bn in public funds for the initiative, but the Senate decision has now placed the funding in jeopardy.

## Key figures

- **Proposed federal allocation**: $1bn in public funds for Secret Service security upgrades
