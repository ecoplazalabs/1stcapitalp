## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market sponsors and operators, elevated UK yields compress valuation multiples and increase debt service costs on refinancing facilities. Cross-border acquirers targeting UK assets face reduced leverage capacity and tighter EBITDA coverage covenants. This environment particularly constrains higher-leverage sponsor deals and suggests a tactical window for fixed-rate lock-in before Friday's anticipated volatility hits pricing.
