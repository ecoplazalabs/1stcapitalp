## What happened

South Africa's World Cup 2026 squad has been unable to depart as scheduled due to visa processing delays for Mexico. The team faces its opening match against Mexico in 11 days, according to Al Jazeera Economy.

## Key figures

- **Match countdown:** 11 days until South Africa's opening World Cup game against Mexico
- **Visa status:** Players remain without Mexican visas, preventing delegation departure
