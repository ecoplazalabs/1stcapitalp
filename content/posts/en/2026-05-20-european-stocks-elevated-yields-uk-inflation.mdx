## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Elevated bond yields directly affect the cost of debt financing for lower middle-market businesses pursuing organic growth or acquisition strategies. Cross-border dealmakers and sponsors must factor higher refinancing costs into working capital models and exit assumptions. UK-focused operators and those with sterling-denominated debt face particular exposure to inflation data releases, which inform base rate expectations and borrowing costs in the near term. For CFOs evaluating acquisition targets or managing existing leverage, yield dynamics shape the feasibility and returns profile of transactions in the current environment.
