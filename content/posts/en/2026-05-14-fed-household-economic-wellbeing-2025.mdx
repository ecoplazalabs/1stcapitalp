## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Consumer household financial health directly influences demand patterns for lower middle-market operators and affects the risk profile of portfolio companies. Understanding macro conditions around household debt, savings rates and employment stability informs commercial credit availability and end-market resilience for $10M–$100M revenue businesses. For PE sponsors evaluating B2B or B2C targets, this report provides baseline data on consumer spending capacity and credit constraints that may impact valuation assumptions and exit multiples.
