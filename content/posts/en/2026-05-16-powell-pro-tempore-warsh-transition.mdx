## What happened

The Federal Reserve Board has appointed Jerome Powell to serve as chair pro tempore, a temporary role he will hold until Kevin Warsh assumes the position of chair following his swearing-in.

## Key figures

- **Interim role:** Jerome Powell named chair pro tempore
- **Successor:** Kevin M. Warsh to be sworn in as new chair
- **Timeline:** Powell's tenure extends until Warsh's swearing-in

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Transitional leadership at the Federal Reserve can create near-term policy uncertainty affecting interest rates and credit availability. For lower middle-market operators and sponsors, shifts in Fed leadership may signal changes to monetary policy stance and capital markets conditions. Clarity on the succession timeline—with Powell maintaining continuity until Warsh takes office—provides some stability for financing decisions and acquisition planning over the coming months.
