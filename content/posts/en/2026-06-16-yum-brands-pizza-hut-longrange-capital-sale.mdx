## What happened

Yum Brands has completed the sale of Pizza Hut to a consortium comprising private equity firm LongRange Capital and Yum China. The transaction values Pizza Hut at $2.7 billion and represents a strategic exit for Yum following prolonged difficulties at the pizza division.

## Key figures

- **Transaction value:** $2.7 billion
- **Buyer consortium:** LongRange Capital and Yum China
- **Asset divested:** Pizza Hut chain

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

This transaction underscores the role private equity firms play in acquiring and restructuring underperforming restaurant and hospitality assets at scale. For lower middle-market operators and sponsors, the deal illustrates both the strategic rationale for carving out non-core divisions and the attractiveness of mature QSR assets to PE investors willing to pursue operational turnarounds. The involvement of Yum China as a co-buyer also demonstrates how regional operators increasingly participate in larger acquisitions, a pattern relevant to sponsors evaluating partnerships in cross-border M&A.
