## What happened

Republicans are struggling to develop a coherent strategy to address elevated price levels ahead of upcoming midterm elections. The party faces a messaging vacuum on inflation as the White House pursues alternative spending priorities.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Inflation dynamics and fiscal policy direction remain critical variables for lower middle-market operators and their sponsors. Uncertainty around the political consensus on price pressures affects working capital planning, debt servicing assumptions, and cross-border transaction valuations. The absence of coherent policy messaging from the governing party may prolong inflation volatility, complicating medium-term planning horizons for M&A and refinancing activities.
