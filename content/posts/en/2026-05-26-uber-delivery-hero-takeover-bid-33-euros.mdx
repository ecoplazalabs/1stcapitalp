## What happened

Delivery Hero confirmed receipt of a takeover offer from Uber at €33 per share. The announcement coincided with a 10% rise in Delivery Hero's share price, indicating market confidence in the valuation or expectation of further bids.

## Key figures

- **Offer price:** €33 per share
- **Share price movement:** +10% following the announcement

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

This development underscores continued consolidation pressure in logistics and delivery platforms, a sector increasingly relevant to lower middle-market operators seeking growth through add-on acquisitions or exit optionality. For sponsors backing similar food service or last-mile delivery businesses, the €33/share benchmark signals valuation momentum in platform businesses with recurring revenue models. Cross-border strategic bidding—particularly from US-listed entities pursuing European assets—remains a key driver of mid-market M&A velocity and pricing power in this vertical.
