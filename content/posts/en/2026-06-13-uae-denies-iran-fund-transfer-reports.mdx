## What happened
The UAE has issued a statement dismissing reports concerning the transfer of funds to Iran. The denial follows a Reuters report claiming the UAE had agreed to release billions of dollars in frozen Iranian assets.

## Key figures
- **Reported frozen funds**: Billions of dollars (per Reuters report)
- **Source of denial**: UAE official statement
- **Timing of denial**: 13 June 2026

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market
For cross-border operators and sponsors with exposure to Gulf markets or Iranian trade relationships, geopolitical developments affecting capital flows and sanctions regimes carry material implications for transaction structuring and counterparty risk assessment. Currency volatility and fund availability in the region can affect working capital management and deal timelines for firms with Middle Eastern operations or partnerships. The UAE's explicit denial underscores the sensitivity around asset controls and financial flows that directly influence dealmaking in emerging markets.
