## What happened

The Federal Reserve Board has approved merger and acquisition applications filed by Columbia Bank MHC and Columbia Financial, Inc. The decision represents a regulatory clearance for the proposed banking combination.

## Key figures

No specific financial metrics, transaction value, or timeline details were disclosed in the announcement.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Banking sector consolidation at this scale can reshape credit availability and terms for lower middle-market borrowers. Changes in bank lending standards, particularly for acquisition financing and working capital facilities, often follow major regulatory approvals. LMM operators and sponsors should monitor how the combined entity adjusts its commercial lending strategy, as regional bank mergers frequently result in portfolio rationalisation that affects non-strategic lending categories.
