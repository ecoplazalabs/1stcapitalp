## What happened

Medallia has experienced significant financial distress, resulting in an equity wipeout and debt becoming distressed. The situation underscores emerging challenges within private credit markets and the quality of underlying portfolio companies.

## Key figures

- **Company**: Medallia
- **Outcome**: Equity wipeout and distressed debt position

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

This development carries direct implications for mid-market operators and sponsors. Private credit providers are increasingly active lenders to businesses in the $10M–$100M revenue range, and portfolio stress signals warrant attention regarding lender underwriting standards, covenant enforcement, and the broader risk environment. Lower middle-market borrowers should expect tighter credit conditions and more rigorous due diligence as lenders reassess their exposure across portfolios.
