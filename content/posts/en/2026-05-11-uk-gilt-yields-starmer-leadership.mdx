## What happened

UK government bonds declined as Prime Minister Starmer encountered political difficulty following election results. The Prime Minister indicated willingness to contest any leadership challenge that emerges from the electoral outcome.

## Key figures

No specific yield, spread or duration figures were disclosed in the source material.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Political instability in the UK affects the risk-free rate environment upon which lower middle-market financing is priced. Rising gilt yields typically compress pricing for sponsor-backed acquisitions and refinancings, whilst weakening sterling sentiment may impact cross-border transactions involving UK targets or sponsors. For merchant finance professionals, elevated sovereign yields and political uncertainty often coincide with widened credit spreads, tightening leverage multiples available to mid-market borrowers.
