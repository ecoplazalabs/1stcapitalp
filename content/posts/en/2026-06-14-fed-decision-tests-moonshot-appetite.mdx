## What happened

SpaceX has demonstrated that investors remain willing to deploy capital into ambitious, early-stage ventures. However, the Federal Reserve's upcoming decision this week presents a potential test of whether this risk appetite can withstand higher interest rates.

## Key figures

No specific financial figures were disclosed in the source material regarding SpaceX's funding round or Fed policy parameters.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market operators and their sponsors, Fed policy directly influences the cost of capital and the relative attractiveness of growth-stage investments. Higher interest rates typically compress valuation multiples for high-growth businesses, which could affect exit multiples and debt availability for acquisitions. The tension between continued investor appetite for moonshot ventures and tighter monetary policy may reshape the risk-reward calculus for cross-border M&A and leveraged buyouts in the current cycle.
