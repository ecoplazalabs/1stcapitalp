## What happened

The European Central Bank has published its indicative operational calendars for 2028. This document provides forward guidance on the timing and scheduling of ECB operations and related market infrastructure activities.

## Key figures

No specific numerical data on monetary policy rates, economic forecasts, or transaction volumes was disclosed in the announcement.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Operational calendars from the ECB serve as essential reference points for cross-border European transactions and financing activities. For lower middle-market sponsors and operators conducting M&A, refinancing, or capital raises involving eurozone counterparties, clarity on ECB operational schedules—including settlement cycles, reporting deadlines, and payment system availability—directly affects deal timing and cash flow management. Early publication of 2028 calendars allows finance teams to plan transaction closings and liquidity events with greater certainty.
