## What happened

UK government borrowing costs have surged to their highest levels in over a century, driven by political uncertainty surrounding the Prime Minister's position. Cabinet ministers have publicly questioned the leadership, creating volatility in the fixed income market.

## Key figures

- **Thirty-year gilt yield:** reached highest level this century

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Political instability and rising gilt yields typically signal broader economic uncertainty that affects lending conditions across the UK. For lower middle-market borrowers and sponsors pursuing acquisitions or refinancing, movements in government bond yields directly influence the cost of capital, bank lending spreads, and the attractiveness of UK assets relative to continental European alternatives. Rising risk premiums driven by sovereign uncertainty can tighten credit availability and increase cost of debt for non-investment-grade borrowers in the £10M–£100M revenue band.
