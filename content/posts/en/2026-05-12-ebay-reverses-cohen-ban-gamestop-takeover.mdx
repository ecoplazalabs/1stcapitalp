## What happened

eBay has reversed its permanent ban on Ryan Cohen's account whilst simultaneously rejecting a takeover attempt by GameStop. The e-commerce platform's decision comes after Cohen's account, which featured GameStop merchandise and other high-value items, had been subject to a permanent suspension.

## Key figures

- **Maximum listing value**: Items on Cohen's account were listed for as much as $14,000
- **Account status**: Permanently banned, then reinstated by eBay

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

This development signals how founder-level disputes and high-profile account actions can intersect with broader M&A activity in consumer-facing businesses. For lower middle-market operators with e-commerce exposure, the episode underscores the regulatory and operational risks inherent in marketplace-dependent revenue models. Cross-border acquirers may also note the implications of centralised platform governance on business continuity and founder involvement post-transaction.
