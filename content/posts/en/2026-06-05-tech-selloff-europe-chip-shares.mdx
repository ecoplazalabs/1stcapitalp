## What happened

A sell-off in technology stocks that originated from Broadcom's earnings report has spread across global markets, reaching European exchanges. Chip shares have experienced notable declines as the downturn extends beyond initial US and Asian trading sessions.

## Key figures

- **Geographic spread**: Sell-off began in US and Asian markets before reaching Europe
- **Trigger event**: Broadcom earnings report earlier in the week
- **Sector impact**: Technology stocks, particularly chip shares

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Global technology sector volatility can create both challenges and opportunities for lower middle-market operators and sponsors. Companies with exposure to semiconductor supply chains, software licensing, or tech-enabled services may face valuation pressure in the near term. Conversely, strategic acquirers with dry powder may find attractive entry points in this environment, whilst cross-border M&A involving European tech assets could benefit from compressed multiples.
