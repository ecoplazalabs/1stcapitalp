## What happened

Kevin Warsh was sworn in as chairman of the Federal Reserve's Board of Governors on 22 May 2026. The Federal Open Market Committee unanimously selected Warsh as its chairman in the same ceremony.

## Key figures

- **Position**: Chairman of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System
- **Committee role**: FOMC chairman (unanimously selected)
- **Date of oath**: 22 May 2026

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

The appointment of a new Federal Reserve chairman signals potential shifts in monetary policy, interest rate trajectories and credit availability that directly affect financing conditions for lower middle-market enterprises. Private equity sponsors and cross-border acquirers rely heavily on stable policy signals; a leadership transition at the Fed warrants close monitoring of future guidance on rates, which influences both the cost of debt financing and valuation multiples for M&A targets in the $10M–$100M revenue range.
