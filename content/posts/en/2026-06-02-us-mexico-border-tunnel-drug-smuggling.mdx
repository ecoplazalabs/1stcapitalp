## What happened

Authorities discovered an underground tunnel connecting Tijuana and San Diego, reportedly used for smuggling drugs and weapons across the US-Mexico border. The discovery highlights persistent security challenges along one of the world's most significant trade corridors.

## Key figures

- **Location:** Tijuana to San Diego underground route
- **Cargo:** Drugs and weapons (alleged)

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market operators with supply chains crossing the US-Mexico border, border security incidents underscore the operational and reputational risks of inadequate due diligence on logistics partners. Cross-border M&A and merchant finance transactions involving companies with Mexican or Central American operations require heightened scrutiny of compliance frameworks and supply chain controls. These discoveries reinforce the need for robust vendor vetting and customs compliance procedures when financing or acquiring businesses dependent on north-south trade routes.
