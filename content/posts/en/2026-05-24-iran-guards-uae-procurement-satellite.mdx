## What happened

Financial Times records show that Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps operated a procurement network through a company based in the United Arab Emirates to purchase military satellite equipment. This sourcing arrangement predated subsequent Iranian missile and drone attacks against the UAE.

## Key figures

No specific financial, quantitative or temporal figures are disclosed in the source material.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

This incident underscores material risks in cross-border transactions involving Gulf suppliers and end-use verification protocols. Lower middle-market firms with supply chain exposure to the region—particularly those in dual-use technology, logistics, or procurement intermediation—should reassess counterparty screening and sanctions compliance frameworks. The use of shell entities for procuring sensitive equipment highlights the importance of enhanced due diligence on ultimate beneficial ownership, particularly where political or geopolitical instability exists.
