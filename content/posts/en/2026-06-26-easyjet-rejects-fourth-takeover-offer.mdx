## What happened

EasyJet has rejected a fourth takeover offer. The airline's board expressed concerns about the "deliverability" of the proposal, indicating doubts about whether the bidder can execute the transaction as presented.

## Key figures

No financial figures were disclosed in the source material regarding the offer or valuation.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Repeated bid rejections on deliverability grounds signal a common friction point in larger M&A processes: the gap between offered price and execution capability. For lower middle-market operators and sponsors evaluating strategic sales or acquisition opportunities, this highlights the importance of thorough due diligence on bidder financing and structural feasibility before engaging in extended negotiation cycles. Multiple rejections can also signal weakening negotiating leverage, a dynamic relevant to any business considering sale timing or competing proposals.
