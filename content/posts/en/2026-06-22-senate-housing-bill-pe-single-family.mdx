## What happened

Both chambers of Congress are expected to advance a housing package this week that addresses supply constraints and affordability. The legislation includes measures aimed at restricting private equity purchases of single-family homes.

## Key figures

No specific financial metrics or quantified policy measures are disclosed in the available source material.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market sponsors and PE firms with residential real estate exposure, legislative restrictions on single-family home acquisition represent a material shift in deal sourcing and portfolio strategy. Firms with existing residential holdings or planned entries into this segment should monitor the bill's passage and final terms, as regulatory constraints could affect valuation multiples, exit timing, and the competitive landscape for acquisitions. Cross-border investors should also note that such restrictions may indirectly influence availability of alternative asset classes as capital seeks new deployment avenues.
