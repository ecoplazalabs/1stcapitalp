## What happened

The 2-year Treasury note yield reached its highest point since February 2025. Concurrently, the 10-year Treasury note yield—the primary benchmark for U.S. government borrowing—rose by four basis points to 4.491%.

## Key figures

- **10-year Treasury yield:** 4.491% (up 4 basis points)
- **2-year Treasury yield:** highest level since February 2025

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Treasury yield movements directly influence the cost of capital for lower middle-market borrowers. Rising yields typically translate to higher interest rates on bank facilities, vendor financing, and acquisition debt—key funding sources for $10M–$100M revenue firms. CFOs and sponsors tracking refinancing windows or preparing for M&A should monitor these benchmarks closely, as upward shifts may compress leverage multiples and alter deal economics. The movement also signals market expectations around inflation and monetary policy, both material to working capital costs and credit spreads in the merchant finance space.
