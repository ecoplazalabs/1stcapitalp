## What happened

MarketWatch analysis examines the sustainability of the current bull market under Federal Reserve Chair Warsh, suggesting that rate-hiking cycles may not necessarily curtail equity gains. The commentary draws on historical patterns to assess whether the threat of monetary tightening poses a genuine threat to continued market strength.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market operators and their financial sponsors, the trajectory of Fed policy directly influences cost of capital, refinancing schedules and valuation multiples. If historical rate-hike cycles do permit equity appreciation, this suggests M&A activity and leverage availability may remain more resilient than conventional wisdom would imply. Cross-border acquirers and growth-stage portfolio companies should monitor Fed communications closely, as the distinction between rate-hike threats and actual tightening could materially affect financing conditions and deal economics over the coming 12–24 months.

> "Warsh may hope the threat of rate hikes is enough. But stocks might gain ground if he does, and past rate-hike cycles can be a guide." — MarketWatch
