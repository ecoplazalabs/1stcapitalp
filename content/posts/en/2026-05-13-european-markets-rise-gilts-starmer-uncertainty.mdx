## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market operators and sponsors with cross-border exposure to continental Europe or the UK, the relationship between equity market direction and gilt market stability carries direct implications for refinancing costs and working capital availability. Political uncertainty in a major economy can create volatility in longer-dated sterling funding, affecting the cost of acquisition financing and dividend recapitalisation structures. Investors monitoring UK-denominated debt or considering GBP-based transactions should track both political developments and corresponding movements in the gilt curve closely.
