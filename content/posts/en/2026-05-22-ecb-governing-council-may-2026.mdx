## What happened

The ECB Governing Council held a meeting on 22 May 2026 to deliberate on decisions extending beyond interest rate adjustments. The official announcement indicates the Council addressed supplementary policy matters as part of its regular governance cycle.

## Key figures

- **Meeting date:** 22 May 2026
- **Decision scope:** Matters beyond interest rate setting
- **Governing body:** ECB Governing Council

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

ECB framework decisions can influence cross-border financing costs and currency volatility for lower middle-market firms operating across eurozone jurisdictions. Changes to monetary policy architecture or reserve requirements may affect borrowing conditions and working capital management for LMM sponsors and operators with European exposure. Clarity on non-rate policy decisions helps inform hedging strategies and refinancing timelines for portfolio companies with EUR-denominated debt.
