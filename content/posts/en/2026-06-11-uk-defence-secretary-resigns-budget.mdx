## What happened

John Healey, UK Defence Secretary, has resigned over disagreements regarding defence spending allocation. Healey stated that the Prime Minister was "unable" and the Treasury "unwilling" to provide the necessary budget for defence priorities.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Government budget constraints and fiscal policy tensions can influence the broader economic environment for M&A and corporate finance activity.
