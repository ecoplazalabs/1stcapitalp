## What happened

Oil prices declined to three-month lows after reports suggested the US was approaching a deal with Iran. The development prompted broad-based gains in both equity markets and government bond prices, reflecting market expectations that energy cost inflation could ease.

## Key figures

- **Oil price level:** Three-month low
- **Market impact:** Stocks and government bonds made significant gains

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Energy cost dynamics directly influence working capital requirements and input costs for lower middle-market operators across manufacturing, logistics, and distribution. A potential resolution to US-Iran tensions could reduce energy price volatility, improving earnings predictability for portfolio companies and strengthening operational margins. For cross-border M&A participants and sponsors, moderating energy costs support valuation multiples by reducing discount rates embedded in bond yield assumptions and improving comparable company metrics.
