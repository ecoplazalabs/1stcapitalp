## What happened

Oil prices reached a three-month low following reports that the US is approaching a deal with Iran. The prospect of eased geopolitical tension in energy markets has prompted a broader rally in both equities and government bonds.

## Key figures

- **Oil price movement:** Touched three-month lows
- **Market reaction:** Stocks and government bonds both made gains

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market operators with exposure to energy costs or commodity-linked revenues, softer oil prices present a potential tailwind to margins and working capital management. The concurrent rally in government bonds signals shifting risk sentiment, which may influence borrowing costs and refinancing opportunities for mid-market sponsors and acquisition-stage companies. Cross-border M&A activity, particularly in energy-dependent sectors, could benefit from reduced macro uncertainty and lower funding costs if the geopolitical de-escalation holds.
