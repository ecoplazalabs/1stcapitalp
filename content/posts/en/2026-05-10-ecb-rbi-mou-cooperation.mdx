## What happened

The European Central Bank and the Reserve Bank of India have formalised a cooperation framework through the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding. The agreement establishes a basis for enhanced engagement between the two institutions on matters of mutual interest in monetary policy, financial stability and central banking operations.

## Key figures

No specific quantitative metrics were disclosed in the announcement.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market operators and cross-border PE sponsors, this formalisation of ECB-RBI cooperation signals improved institutional coordination between two of Asia and Europe's most influential central banks. Enhanced dialogue on monetary policy and financial stability can reduce uncertainty in currency markets and cross-border capital flows—material considerations for lower middle-market firms executing India-EU transactions or managing multi-currency exposure. The framework may also facilitate smoother regulatory engagement for European financial institutions seeking to expand operations or investment exposure in India.
