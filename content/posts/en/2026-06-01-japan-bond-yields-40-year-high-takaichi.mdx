## What happened

Japanese bond yields have reached their highest levels in 40 years, according to CNBC World. Prime Minister Takaichi's recent budget remarks have raised concerns amongst fixed income investors about the trajectory of fiscal policy.

## Key figures

- **Yield milestone**: Japanese bond yields at highest levels in 40 years

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market operators and sponsors with exposure to Japanese operations or cross-border M&A involving Japanese assets, rising yields signal higher borrowing costs and potentially compressed valuations in the region. Institutional investors evaluating Japanese-linked opportunities should monitor fiscal policy signals closely, as yield moves of this magnitude typically reflect broader macroeconomic repricing that affects leverage availability and cost of capital across markets.
