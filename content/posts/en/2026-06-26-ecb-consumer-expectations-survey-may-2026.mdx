## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Consumer sentiment data influences ECB monetary policy decisions, which directly affect borrowing costs for lower middle-market operators. Shifts in household inflation expectations can signal demand pressures or deflationary risks, both material to pricing power and working capital management for SME sponsors and their portfolio companies. Cross-border M&A activity in the eurozone is sensitive to ECB policy shifts signalled through consumer data.
