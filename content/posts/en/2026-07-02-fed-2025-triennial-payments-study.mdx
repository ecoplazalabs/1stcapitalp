## What happened

The Federal Reserve released initial findings from its 2025 triennial payments study on 1 July 2026. The study provides a comprehensive review of payment system developments and usage patterns across the United States.

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

Payment system trends directly influence cash management, working capital efficiency, and operational costs for lower middle-market businesses. Understanding how consumer and commercial payment preferences are evolving helps CFOs optimise their treasury operations and manage client receivables more effectively. For sponsors evaluating acquisition targets, shifting payment behaviours can signal changes in customer engagement patterns and potential working capital requirements.
