## What happened

Ovo Energy is undergoing a planned takeover that is expected to create one of Britain's largest energy suppliers. The deal includes a commitment to honour all existing customer tariffs in their entirety.

## Key figures

- **Deal scope**: Creation of one of Britain's largest energy suppliers
- **Customer protection**: All existing tariffs to be honoured in full

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

This transaction exemplifies how consolidation in regulated utilities can restructure competitive landscapes. For lower middle-market operators in the energy sector or those considering acquisition within utilities, the emphasis on customer tariff protection signals regulatory priorities around consumer safeguarding during M&A. Cross-border sponsors evaluating UK energy assets should note the stringent conditions applied to major sector consolidations, which may influence deal structure, valuation multiples and integration planning for similar transactions.
