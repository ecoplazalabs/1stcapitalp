## What happened

Cattle markets in West Bengal have experienced significant disruption as tensions rise surrounding Eid al-Adha preparations. This disruption follows a notable political shift in the state, where the BJP secured electoral victory for the first time in May 2026.

## Key figures

- **Geographic focus:** West Bengal, India
- **Event:** BJP electoral victory in May 2026 (first time)
- **Religious observance:** Eid al-Adha celebrations
- **Market impact:** Cattle market emptying reported
