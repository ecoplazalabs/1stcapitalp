## What happened

Meta has entered into an agreement with Reliance Industries, owned by Mukesh Ambani, to lease AI-enabled data centre capacity in India. The deal represents Meta's latest infrastructure expansion in the region as the hyperscaler builds out computational resources to support AI workloads.

## Key figures

- **Partner:** Reliance Industries (Mukesh Ambani)
- **Geography:** India
- **Infrastructure type:** AI-enabled data centre lease

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

This infrastructure play underscores the accelerating deployment of AI compute capacity in emerging markets, particularly India. For lower middle-market operators and PE sponsors evaluating cross-border expansion, particularly in software, analytics and AI-adjacent services, regional data residency and latency advantages will increasingly influence site selection and go-to-market strategy. The movement of hyperscaler capex into India also signals maturing regulatory frameworks and commercial conditions that could favour mid-market technology and data-intensive businesses seeking to establish regional hubs or serve local enterprise demand.
