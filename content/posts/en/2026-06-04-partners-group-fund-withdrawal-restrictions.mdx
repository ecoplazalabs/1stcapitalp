## What happened

Partners Group has indicated it may impose withdrawal restrictions on more of its funds, following gating measures on European vehicles that prompted a private equity market decline. The firm's action reflects broader liquidity pressures affecting the private equity sector.

## Key figures

- **Date of announcement:** 4 June 2026
- **Scope:** Potential restrictions across additional funds beyond those already gated
- **Market impact:** Private equity stock sell-off triggered on Wednesday

## Why it matters for the lower middle-market

For lower middle-market sponsors and their LP base, Partners Group's moves signal tightening liquidity conditions in continuation vehicles and continuation funds—structures often used to extend hold periods in sub-€100M deals. Gating restrictions limit investor access to capital and may compress denominator effects for LPs, whilst signalling that dry powder shortfalls are spreading beyond single funds. Mid-market operators should monitor whether their own fund sponsors face similar pressures, as redemption caps can delay exit proceeds and refinancing access.
